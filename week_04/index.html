Baked Apple Fritters

Recipe Overview

Yields: 14 servings  |  Prep time: 20 minutes  |  Total Time: 1 hour 15 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 3/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 tsp. baking powder
  • 1 1/2 tsp. ground cinnamon
  • 1 tsp. kosher salt + a pinch for glaze
  • 1/4 tsp. finely grated fresh nutmeg
  • 6 tbsp. unsalted butter, cold, cubed
  • 2 medium Granny Smith apples, peeled, cored, finely chopped
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1 tsp. pure vanilla extract
  • 1/4 cup milk (for glaze)
  • 2 cups confectioners sugar (for glaze)

Directions

First, gather the ingredients for the fritters. Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C) and then gather 2 baking sheets. Line them with parchment paper. In a big bowl, mix the flour, sugar, baking powder, cinnamon, salt, and nutmeg together well. Add the butter and work it into the mixture, using your hands to break the butter down into pea-sized pieces. Add the apple pieces into the bowl and coat them well. Get a small bowl and mix the eggs, cream, and vanilla together. Add the small bowl to big bowl and mix until the batter becomes lumpy and sticky. Scoop the batter onto the baking sheets using a 1/4-cup measuring cup or a large cookie scoop. Bake the batter for 12-15 minutes until the tops are slightly golden and the edges are firm, rotating the baking sheets top to bottom halfway through. Let the fritters cool for 5 minutes, and then transfer them to a wire rack to cool completely.

Next, gather the ingredients for the glaze. Get a medium bowl and stir the confectioners sugar, milk, and salt until it runs off your spoon easily. Add more milk if needed, 1 teaspoon at a time, until the glaze is thin enough to dip the fritters in. Once the fritters are ready, dip the tops of them into the glaze and place them back on the rack. Wait approximately 10 minutes for the glaze to harden.

Pumpkin Pie

Recipe Overview

Yields: 6 servings  |  Prep time: 30 minutes  |  Total Time: 2 hours 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • Cooking spray
  • 1 tbsp. all-purpose flour + more for rolling
  • 1 batch pie crust, chilled
  • 3 eggs, beaten to blend
  • 1 can (15oz.) pumpkin puree
  • 3/4 cup packed brown sugar
  • 1 1/4 cups heavy cream
  • 2 tsp. pumpkin pie spice
  • 1 tsp. pure vanilla extract
  • 1/4 tsp. kosher salt
  • Whipped cream, for serving (optional)

Directions

First, preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C) and place a backing sheet inside. Get a 9" x 1.5" pie dish and lightly grease it with the cooking spray. Spread some flour on a surface and roll dough to approximately a 12" circle. Drape the dough over the pie dish and gently press it down to fit it in, eliminating all air bubbles. Trim the overhang to 1" and tuck it under itself. Crimp the edges of the dough as desired. Place it in the fridge until it's cold. Minimum 30 minutes; maximum 3 hours. Line the dough with foil/parchment and fill it with pie weights. Bake the crust until the top and sides are dry and set (15-18 minutes), and then remove the foil and pie weights. Continue baking in the oven until the bottom is dry (~5 minutes). Let it cool down before reducing the oven to 350°F (175°C).

Get a large bowl and whisk together the eggs, pumpkin, brown sugar, cream, pumpkin pie spice, vanilla, salt, and flour until the mixture is smooth. Pour it into the crust, and then gently place the pie dish on a heated sheet. Bake the pie until the filling is a little jiggly in the middle and the crust is golden brown (45-50 minutes). Switch off the oven and prop open the door to let the pie cool for 30 minutes. Then, transfer the pie dish to a wire rack to let it cool completely. Serve with whipped cream if desired.

Mini Black Forest Trifles

Recipe Overview

Yields: 12 servings  |  Prep time: 15 minutes  |  Total Time: 2 hours

Ingredients

  • Cooking spray
  • 1 (13.25 oz) box chocolate cake mix + ingredients called for on box
  • 3 tbsp. kirsch cherry liqueur, divided (optional)
  • 1 (3 oz) box instant chocolate pudding
  • 2 cups milk
  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 1/4 cup (30 g) confectioners sugar
  • 1 (13 oz) jar cherry preserves
  • Chocolate shavings, for serving
  • Cake tester

Directions

Arrange a rack in the centre of the oven and preheat it to 350°F (175°C). Get a 13" x 9" baking pan and it grease it with cooking spray. Get a large bowl and make the cake mix according to the instructions on the box. Then, pour the batter into the baking pan. Place the pan inside the over and wait until the batter is baked fully (20-25 minutes). Check this by inserting the cake tester into the centre of the cake and pulling it out to see if any batter hasn't baked. Use a fork to poke the cake all over, and then brush 2 tbsp of liqueur over the top (if using). Let the cake cool. Cut approximately half of the cake into 1/2" pieces and reserve the remaining cake for another use.

Get a medium bowl and whisk together the pudding mix and the milk until it starts to thicken. Place into the fridge until it's ready to be used. Get a large bowl and place in the cream and sugar. Beat them with a handheld mixer on medium-high speed until medium peaks form. Get a small bowl and combine the cherry preserves and the remaining 1 tbsp. of kirsch (if using). Gather small glasses or jars and place 4-5 cake pieces to cover the bottom. top the cake pieces with whipped cream, the preserves mixture, and then chocolate pudding. Repeat the layers again, and then top the glasses with another spoonful of whipped cream and chocolate shavings. Place into the fridge until they're ready to be served.

Apple Cider Donuts

Recipe Overview

Yields: 12 servings  |  Prep time: 10 minutes  |  Total Time: 1 hour

Ingredients

  • Cooking spray
  • 1 cup apple cider
  • 2 cups (240 g) all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp. baking powder
  • 1 tsp. kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp. ground nutmeg
  • 1/4 tsp ground ginger
  • 3 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon, divided
  • 3/4 cup vegetable oil
  • 1/2 cup (105 g) packed dark brown sugar
  • 1 cup (200 g) granulated sugar, divided
  • 1 egg
  • 6 tbsp. unsalted butter, melted
  • 2 (6-cavity) donut pans

Directions

Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease the 2 donut pans with cooking spray. Get a small pot, pour in the apple cider, and place it on the stove on medium heat. Bring the apple cider to a boil and cook until it's reduced to approximately 1/2 cup (10-15 minutes). Then, let it cool down. While the apple cider is cooking, get a medium bowl and whisk together the flour, baking powder, salt, nutmeg, ginger, and 2 tsp cinnamon. Get a large bowl and whisk together the oil, brown sugar, and 1/2 cup granulated sugar. Add the egg and whisk until it's fullt incorperated in the mixture. Add the dry ingredients and the apple cider and whisk right until it's combined.

Once you have your batter, transfer it to a piping or plastic bag with a small corner snipped off. Pipe the batter into the donut pans (or scoop the batter in with a spoon) until each cavity is approximately 3/4 full. Place the pans in the oven and bake the donuts until the tops are dry (12-15 minutes). Let them cool for 5 minutes, and then place the pans onto a wire rack. Get a wide shallow bowl and combine the remaining 1/2 cup granulated sugar and 1 1/2 tsp cinnamon. Brush the donuts with butter while they're still warm and sprinkle the cinnamon sugar on top of each donut.
